StreamHolt

Download non-DRM streams right in your browser — no companion app, no server, no account.

Chrome Web Store — coming soon

What it does

  • One-click detection — spots HLS, DASH, and direct MP4 media playing in the current tab and shows quality, duration, and estimated size.
  • Everything happens locally — segment download and merging (ffmpeg.wasm) run inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Proper audio — separate audio/video renditions are muxed together, so you never end up with a silent file.
  • Subtitles — downloads WebVTT subtitle tracks alongside the video.
  • Live recording — record an HLS live stream and save what you have when you stop. Free records up to 5 minutes, Pro has no time limit.
  • Audio extraction, clipping, audio-track choice (Pro) — save M4A/MP3, keep only a time range, or pick the audio language.

What it deliberately does not do

YouTube is blocked at the code level and DRM-protected streams (Widevine, SAMPLE-AES) are rejected — StreamHolt is built for legitimately accessible, non-DRM media such as your own hosted videos, course platforms, and embeds you are entitled to save. Always respect each site's terms of service and copyright law.

Privacy

StreamHolt collects no data. Detection, downloading, and merging never leave your device. Activating Pro sends only a license key and a random device id — never media or browsing data. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Pricing

Detection, downloads, original quality, and subtitles stay free and unlimited — no daily quota, no resolution cap, no watermark, no trial, no account. Free runs one download at a time and records live for 5 minutes.
Pro lifetime license — launch price $14, one-time (regular $19), 3 devices: unlimited live recording, 3 concurrent downloads, audio extraction, clipping, and audio-track choice.
