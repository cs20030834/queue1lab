SnapHolt

Capture now. Watch what changes.

Install from the Chrome Web Store — free

SnapHolt is the current product name. LongSnap was an earlier, unpublished name and is now retired.

What it does

  • Full-page capture — capture long pages and selected areas, with support for common complex page layouts.
  • Annotate & export — refine captures with editing tools and save them in common image and document formats.
  • Verifiable records — keep capture context and integrity information, with optional tools for later verification.
  • Batch capture — collect multiple pages in one run and organize the results for repeated work.
  • 💥 Destroy & Capture — compose playful page effects, then save the result as a screenshot.
  • Scheduled capture & change detection — schedule captures on your device, compare selected areas, and revisit results in local history while Chrome is available.

The editor with a full-page capture — the record band stores URL, time, and hash for later integrity checks.

Destroy & Capture: effects stick to the page; use Capture result to save them.

Privacy

Core capture, editing, schedules, and history run on your device. License activation sends limited license details to Polar; the optional Trusted Timestamp sends only a capture hash to freetsa.org. Captured images, MHTML, page contents, and URLs are not sent to Queue1Lab. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Pricing

Free includes core tools for full-page capture, editing, local history, and playful page effects. Scheduled capture, batch work, and verifiable records are also available within the included limits.
Lifetime (SnapHolt 1.x) — $9.99 one-time, up to 3 devices you own or control: expands usage limits and adds conveniences for frequent work, such as more flexible scheduling, batch workflows, and file organization. Available for purchase from inside the installed extension. Polar shows the final total and applicable tax at checkout. Future roadmap features are not included unless explicitly stated · license terms

Good to know

Scheduled jobs require Chrome and your device to be available and are not a guaranteed 24/7 monitoring service. Local history and settings can be lost if the extension is removed or browser/device data is reset, so export important records. Verifiable records help check integrity but do not guarantee legal admissibility · details

Where SnapHolt is headed

SnapHolt is exploring ways to make recurring documentation and verification easier.
Capture Recipe — save capture areas, delays, exclusions, and output settings.
Change Timeline — review more precise change history while filtering noise such as ads, timestamps, and animation.
If demand is validated, SnapHolt Watch may monitor pages even when your browser is closed and notify you by email or webhook, potentially as a separate service or plan. Direction, not a promise: features, dates, scope, pricing, and availability may change based on feedback and validation.
